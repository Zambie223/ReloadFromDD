### Fixed
- The equipment HUD's counts now include the depot: spare ammo for the rifle, rebar gun and nobelisk detonator, chainsaw fuel, and the fuel and filter counts for the jetpack, gas mask and hazmat suit. Before, they showed only what was in your inventory (reloading and refuelling from the depot already worked).
