# Edge cases (single player). Run like sp_test.txt.
wait 20
log EDGE START
RFDD.InvFirst 1
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorRifle arms
wait 3

log E1 inventory full, ammo only in the depot. Expect loaded 15, depot 40 -> 25 (taking from the depot needs no inventory space)
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 40
RFDD.Inv Desc_Cement 100000
RFDD.Use empty
RFDD.Use reload
wait 5
RFDD.Use weapon
RFDD.Inv Desc_Cement clear

log E2 depot emptied during the reload timer: inventory 3, depot 10. Expect loaded 3 from the inventory, no partial-remove warning
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 10
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard 3
RFDD.Use empty
RFDD.Use reload
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard -100000
wait 5
RFDD.Use weapon

log E3 not enough for a full magazine: inventory 5, depot 4. Expect loaded 9, inventory 0, depot 0
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 4
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard 5
RFDD.Use empty
RFDD.Use reload
wait 5
RFDD.Use weapon

log E4 healing with an empty depot: hand 1. Expect the hand empties (vanilla)
RFDD.Depot Desc_Berry -100000
RFDD.Equip Desc_Berry arms 1
wait 3
RFDD.Use eat
wait 1
RFDD.Count Desc_Berry

log E5 Healing switched off: depot 10, hand 2. Expect hand 2 -> 1 -> 0, depot stays 10
RFDD.Set Healing false
wait 2
RFDD.Depot Desc_Berry 10
RFDD.Equip Desc_Berry arms 2
wait 3
RFDD.Use eat
wait 1
RFDD.Use eat
wait 1
RFDD.Count Desc_Berry
RFDD.Set Healing true
wait 2

log E6 jetpack with only a non-preferred fuel in the depot: no biofuel anywhere, 5 packaged fuel. Expect Desc_Fuel available; after drain + regen, depot fuel 5 -> 4
RFDD.Inv Desc_Biofuel clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_Biofuel -100000
RFDD.Inv Desc_Fuel clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_Fuel -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_Fuel 5
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorJetPack back
wait 3
RFDD.Use jetpack

wait 8
RFDD.Use jetpack
RFDD.Count Desc_Fuel

log E7 jetpack with nothing anywhere. Expect no fuel types available, no refuel, nothing taken
RFDD.Depot Desc_Fuel -100000
wait 2
RFDD.Use jetpack
RFDD.Use drain
wait 8
RFDD.Use jetpack

RFDD.Calls
log EDGE DONE
