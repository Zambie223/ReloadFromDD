# Dev recon: dumps the bytecode of loaded widget and equipment Blueprints with the rifle, then the chainsaw, equipped,
# to find what the HUD spare-ammo and fuel counters read. Output: <userdir>/Saved/RFDDDump/.
wait 15
log HUDDUMP start
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 50
RFDD.Depot Desc_Biofuel 20
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorRifle arms
wait 4
RFDD.DumpBP widgets
RFDD.DumpBP Equip_
RFDD.Equip Desc_Chainsaw arms
wait 4
RFDD.DumpBP widgets
RFDD.DumpBP Equip_
log HUDDUMP done
wait 2
quit
