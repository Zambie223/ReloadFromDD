# HUD counters with a category switched off, plus the jetpack depot-only refresh (dev build).
# Expected log lines: "[dev] HUD count <widget> <item>: inventory N (category off, depot not added)" while a switch is
# off, and "inventory N + depot M" again once it is back on. RFDD.HudText logs what the HUD slots actually show
# (the jetpack slot gets its count pushed by the jetpack's fuel-amount event, which the HUD count log can't see).
wait 15
log HUDOFF start
RFDD.Status
RFDD.InvFirst 0
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard clear
RFDD.Inv Desc_Biofuel clear
RFDD.Inv Desc_Fuel clear
RFDD.Inv Desc_Filter clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_Biofuel -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_Fuel -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_Filter -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 40
RFDD.Depot Desc_Biofuel 30
RFDD.Depot Desc_Filter 7
log HUDOFF jetpack depot-only refresh: expect a biofuel count, then +20 without touching the inventory
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorJetPack back
wait 4
RFDD.Use jetpack
RFDD.HudText
RFDD.Depot Desc_Biofuel 20
wait 6
RFDD.Use jetpack
RFDD.HudText
log HUDOFF jetpack with JetpackFuel off: expect "inventory 0 (category off)" after the next refresh
RFDD.Set JetpackFuel false
wait 3
RFDD.HudText
RFDD.Depot Desc_Biofuel 1
wait 4
RFDD.HudText
RFDD.Set JetpackFuel true
wait 3
RFDD.HudText
RFDD.Depot Desc_Biofuel 1
wait 4
RFDD.HudText
log HUDOFF rifle: expect 0+40
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorRifle arms
wait 4
log HUDOFF rifle with Ammo off: expect "inventory 0 (category off)", then "inventory 5 (category off)"
RFDD.Set Ammo false
wait 3
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 1
wait 4
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard 5
wait 4
log HUDOFF rifle re-equipped with Ammo off: still inventory only
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorGasmask head
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorRifle arms
wait 4
log HUDOFF rifle with Ammo back on: expect inventory 5 + depot 41
RFDD.Set Ammo true
wait 3
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 1
wait 4
log HUDOFF gas mask with Filters off (timer-driven slot): expect "inventory 0 (category off)", then 0+7 again
RFDD.Set Filters false
wait 4
RFDD.Set Filters true
wait 4
RFDD.HudText
log HUDOFF done
RFDD.Status
wait 2
quit
