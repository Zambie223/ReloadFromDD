# Client half of the multiplayer test (see mp_host.txt). Starts when the client's world begins, which is
# about when the host sees two players; actions sit in the middle of each 30-second slot.
# Client: -userdir=<own folder> -ExecCmds="RFDD.ScriptOnLoad <this file>,open 127.0.0.1:7777"
wait 10
log CLIENT start
RFDD.Status

wait 35

RFDD.Use weapon

wait 2

wait 18

RFDD.Use eat
wait 3

wait 19
log CLIENT slot 3: gas mask charge from the client (ChargeForUse runs locally and on the server)
