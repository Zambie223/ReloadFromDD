# Listen-host half of the multiplayer test. The host sets up the CLIENT's character (RFDD.DevPlayer 1);
# mp_client.txt performs the actions on the client in 30-second slots that start when both players are present.
# Host: -ExecCmds="RFDD.ScriptOnLoad <this file>,open Persistent_Level?loadgame=<save>?listen"
wait 5
waitplayers 2 600
log MP T0 (both players present)
RFDD.Status
RFDD.DevPlayer 1
RFDD.InvFirst 1
wait 30

log MP slot 1 (T0+30): client rifle, depot 40, inventory 0. Client reloads; expect loaded 15, depot 25 on both sides
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorRifle arms
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 40
RFDD.Use empty
wait 25
RFDD.Use weapon
wait 5

log MP slot 2 (T0+60): client berries, hand 2, depot 10. Client eats twice; expect hand 1, depot 9
RFDD.Depot Desc_Berry -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_Berry 10
RFDD.Equip Desc_Berry arms 2
wait 25
RFDD.Count Desc_Berry
wait 5

log MP slot 3 (T0+90): client gas mask, depot 10 filters. Client charges; expect depot 9, client inventory untouched
RFDD.Inv Desc_Filter clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_Filter -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_Filter 10
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorGasmask head
wait 25
RFDD.Count Desc_Filter
wait 5

log MP slot 4 (T0+120): client jetpack, depot 20 packaged fuel (+ the save's biofuel). Server drains at T0+135; expect a refuel from the depot
RFDD.Inv Desc_Fuel clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_Fuel -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_Fuel 20
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorJetPack back
wait 15
RFDD.Use drain
wait 10
RFDD.Use jetpack
wait 5

log MP slot 5 (T0+150): host turns Ammo off; the client must follow (spare = inventory only)
RFDD.DevPlayer 1
RFDD.Equip BP_EquipmentDescriptorRifle arms
RFDD.Inv Desc_CartridgeStandard clear
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard -100000
RFDD.Depot Desc_CartridgeStandard 30
RFDD.Set Ammo false
wait 30
RFDD.Set Ammo true
wait 15
RFDD.Calls
log MP DONE
quit
